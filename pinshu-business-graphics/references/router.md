# Visual Mother Router

Select by the information relationship, not by the topic keyword.

| Content relationship | First choice | Strong alternative | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| one memorable thesis or cover concept | typographic metaphor | Chinese modernism | the page must carry dense evidence |
| positioning, competition, opportunity, risk-return | strategic map | data journalism | the axes cannot be defined honestly |
| capability stack, governance, organization, system foundation | structural section | engineering blueprint narrative | the layers have no dependency or load-bearing relationship |
| industry observation, report opener, mixed documents and images | editorial collage | typographic metaphor | precision or auditability is the primary goal |
| mechanism, operating model, AI workflow, interfaces | engineering blueprint narrative | structural section | the content is only a slogan with no internal mechanism |
| Chinese brand, cultural business, restrained editorial argument | Chinese modernism | typographic metaphor | the content depends on dense charts or global-tech visual cues |
| trends, markets, operating metrics, survey findings | data journalism | strategic map | no verified dataset or source exists |

## Decision questions

Ask internally:

1. What relationship must the viewer understand in three seconds?
2. Does the page need memory, explanation, evidence, or decision support?
3. Is there real data, or only a conceptual claim?
4. Is the structure spatial, sequential, layered, relational, or comparative?
5. Which mother can remove decoration and still preserve the meaning?

## Series routing

Do not create variety by cycling styles arbitrarily.

- Concept-led report: typographic metaphor as opener; strategic map or structural section for analysis.
- AI or systems report: engineering blueprint narrative as opener/mechanism; data journalism for evidence.
- Chinese brand report: Chinese modernism as primary; editorial collage for chapter openers.
- Market report: data journalism as primary; strategic map for positioning.

Keep the shared palette and typography stable when two mothers coexist.

## Selection response format

Return:

- recommended mother
- why it matches the content relationship
- proposed layout
- one primary metaphor, if needed
- biggest visual risk
- whether bitmap generation is suitable for the final deliverable

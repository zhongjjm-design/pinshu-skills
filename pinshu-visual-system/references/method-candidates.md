# Research-derived cultural poster candidates

Two method cards extend cultural-poster research while the original twelve illustration modes retain their existing states. These are separate experimental methods, not two new approved brand modes and not additional Skills.

| Candidate ID | Use | Distinct increment |
|---|---|---|
| oriental-colorfield-landscape | Cultural atmosphere, exhibition or book-poster concept | Derive a field trajectory, small landscape and miniature narrative from the theme; vary structure, not only hue |
| oriental-material-craft | Craft/material cultural-poster concept | Derive the large form from an actual material/action and connect it to the tiny workshop through that same material |

The cards synthesize abstract design hypotheses from user-supplied public poster references and our own experiments. The original external author/source is not yet recovered. No external prompt text, reference image, private feedback or branded artwork is redistributed. They are research-derived methods, not claims of exclusive ownership of a cultural visual style.

Use only with an explicit single-image method test. The compiler supports --method-candidate together with --candidate-test, structure=single-claim and the dedicated xiaohongshu vertical preset. That preset is an internal 3:4 delivery size; it is not a claim that an original reference used that ratio. Neither method enters automatic recommendation or batch routing.

Open the selected card for detailed grammar. Inspect full view, thumbnail, material-action connection, source fit and series change. A method may not be promoted on the strength of a generated file, an inherited liking or a script-level PASS. Recover sources, compare existing methods, test new themes and obtain human acceptance before promotion.

## Current evidence

On 2026-10-06, Material Craft produced two newly inspected examples (papermaking and paper folding) from original fictional source concepts, with exact-size export and clean-copy checks. The material geometry changed between themes. Human acceptance and broader-theme repeatability remain pending. Colorfield Landscape has a prepared method card but no new actual-image trial in this release. These states are recorded explicitly; neither card is promoted.

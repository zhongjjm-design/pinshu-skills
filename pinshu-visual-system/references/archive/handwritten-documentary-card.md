# Handwritten Documentary Card

ID: `handwritten-documentary-card`. Public status: `candidate-frozen`.

## Intent

Let a credible everyday moment carry a short human observation.

## Visual grammar

High-key near-white field; one continuous photographic or documentary scene; sparse handwritten title and one note; one accent color.

## Prompt skeleton

Create a {platform} image for {claim}. Use this visual grammar: High-key near-white field; one continuous photographic or documentary scene; sparse handwritten title and one note; one accent color. Express only the source-supported scene or relationship. Preserve exact names and labels. Reserve whitespace for readable text.

## Avoid

Automatic production; collage of unrelated modules; invented personal experience; generic poster shell.

## Acceptance

Inspect the rendered image at full size and thumbnail size. Check source meaning, exact text, single focal point, natural object/action relationships and platform crop. A registered mode does not establish acceptance of an individual image. Candidate modes require explicit single-image testing; frozen candidates remain blocked.

# Oriental Material and Craft - experimental method

Status: candidate-reference. Scope: one cultural-poster concept. Character: no fixed identity. Content: one real or explicitly imagined material/action, not a dense knowledge process.

## Derive the image

Start with the material and a physically understandable craft action. Identify one shape that action produces: a fibre spread, folded sheet, printed negative, joined crack or overlapping layer. Let that shape carry the large visual, while a very small workshop makes the action discoverable nearby. Connect the two scales through the same material or tool path; the connection needs a visible origin and destination.

The shared language is clean warm handmade paper, restrained rich color, one large material form, active empty space and small flat pale-color figures. Material detail should support the action rather than become random noise. The reading path moves from large form to material, tiny action and brief editorial text. Keep cultural objects plausible and distinguish imagined art direction from historical evidence.

## Change between themes

Change the material-generated geometry, location of the workshop, reading direction and actual action. Do not repeat a fixed lower-row workshop under a recolored blob. Reconsider the large/small scale ratio at thumbnail size; percentages from references are hypotheses, not permanent acceptance thresholds.

## Failure and correction

If the large form could describe any theme, derive it again from the material/action. If the worker does not affect the material, repair the physical connection. If figures look like 3D toys or photographed people, return to flat paper illustration. If text overwhelms the form, reduce secondary wording and use an editable layer for real event information. Reject invented dates, venue, sponsors or heritage credentials.

## Review

Actually inspect the full image and thumbnail. Check one first focal point, discernible craft action, a plausible material connection, clean controlled texture, natural typography and variation across themes. Source match and mechanical export are separate from aesthetic acceptance. Keep the experiment pending until the user confirms it.

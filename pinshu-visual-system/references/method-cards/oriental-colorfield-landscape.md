# Oriental Color Field and Small Landscape - experimental method

Status: candidate-reference. Scope: a single cultural atmosphere or exhibition/book-poster concept. Character: no fixed identity. This method does not explain a mechanism or substitute for an information graphic.

Translate the theme into a spatial direction or tension, then make a dominant pigment/paper field participate in that composition. Place a restrained small landscape and a miniature action where they explain scale or reading direction. Use warm clean paper, controlled saturated mineral-like color and sparse editorial text. Let the distant view establish one large shape, with narrative detail discovered nearby.

Change field trajectory, landscape outline, negative-space cut, viewpoint and micro-story between themes. A riverbank, bridge, wind path or snow gap is a source/theme choice, not a compulsory motif. Avoid recoloring the same black horizontal band, a giant decorative mountain, random aged textures and ornamental pseudo-writing.

At full size check the narrative and source limits; at thumbnail size check the large form and scale hierarchy. For real event details use an editable text layer and verify dates, places and attribution. Keep this method experimental until cross-theme review and human acceptance establish useful boundaries.

# Literary Crayon Editorial

ID: `literary-crayon-editorial`. Public status: `available-scoped`.

## Intent

Give one thesis a memorable symbolic image with literary editorial rhythm.

## Visual grammar

A short heavy display headline, one coarse crayon symbol, broken pigment and deliberate asymmetry on a light field; restrained notes.

## Prompt skeleton

Create a {platform} image for {claim}. Use this visual grammar: A short heavy display headline, one coarse crayon symbol, broken pigment and deliberate asymmetry on a light field; restrained notes. Express only the source-supported scene or relationship. Preserve exact names and labels. Reserve whitespace for readable text.

## Avoid

WeChat covers or article illustrations; dense knowledge; multiple theses; polished corporate clipart.

## Acceptance

Inspect the rendered image at full size and thumbnail size. Check source meaning, exact text, single focal point, natural object/action relationships and platform crop. A registered mode does not establish acceptance of an individual image. Candidate modes require explicit single-image testing; frozen candidates remain blocked.

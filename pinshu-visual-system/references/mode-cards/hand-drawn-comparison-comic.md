# Hand-drawn Comparison Comic

ID: `hand-drawn-comparison-comic`. Public status: `available-scoped`.

## Intent

Make two situations, before/after or two stages readable through actions.

## Visual grammar

Warm white paper, relaxed dark hand lines, muted blue/sage/wood and sparse yellow. Two comparable scenes, one visible relationship, short labels and large whitespace.

## Prompt skeleton

Create a {platform} image for {claim}. Use this visual grammar: Warm white paper, relaxed dark hand lines, muted blue/sage/wood and sparse yellow. Two comparable scenes, one visible relationship, short labels and large whitespace. Express only the source-supported scene or relationship. Preserve exact names and labels. Reserve whitespace for readable text.

## Avoid

More than two main scenes; complex loops; chibi faces; random robot/lightbulb decoration; multiple crossing arrows.

## Acceptance

Inspect the rendered image at full size and thumbnail size. Check source meaning, exact text, single focal point, natural object/action relationships and platform crop. A registered mode does not establish acceptance of an individual image. Candidate modes require explicit single-image testing; frozen candidates remain blocked.

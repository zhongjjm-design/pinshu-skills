# Archive Tabletop Editorial

ID: `archive-tabletop-editorial`. Public status: `available-scoped`.

## Intent

Explain a concept through 4-7 distinct objects and their placement.

## Visual grammar

Top-down research desk; high-key gray-white or pale sage; paper, frosted acrylic, metal and wood; soft top light. Reserve a clean title zone. Objects correspond to named elements; at most one subtle reading path.

## Prompt skeleton

Create a {platform} image for {claim}. Use this visual grammar: Top-down research desk; high-key gray-white or pale sage; paper, frosted acrylic, metal and wood; soft top light. ## Avoid

Complex causality; indistinguishable recolored cubes; product display; fake documents; generated long text.

## Acceptance

Inspect the rendered image at full size and thumbnail size. Check source meaning, exact text, single focal point, natural object/action relationships and platform crop. A registered mode does not establish acceptance of an individual image. Candidate modes require explicit single-image testing; frozen candidates remain blocked.
